Huayi Zheng

I am an incoming PhD student at the British Antarctic Survey and the University of Southampton, starting in September 2026, supported by the IGNITE Doctoral Landscape Award. I will be working with Dr. Andrea Meijers and Dr. Alessandro Silvano. My PhD research focuses on Antarctic Bottom Water (AABW): specifically, how much AABW will slow down in the future and what the impacts of such a slowdown will be.

Previously, I completed my Master's degree at the Institute of Atmospheric Physics, Chinese Academy of Sciences, under the supervision of Prof. Lijing Cheng. My Master's research focused on the contemporary sea-level budget and Atlantic meridional heat and freshwater transport.

Find me also in: Google Scholar, ResearchGate, WeChat, Email (zhenghuayi@mail.iap.ac.cn)
